# SmartMacroAI Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 9, 2026

SmartMacroAI uses Google Sign-In only to authenticate users and associate an account with SmartMacroAI services. The app requests the `openid`, `email`, and `profile` scopes and may receive your Google account identifier, email address, display name, and profile image.

We use this information only for account authentication, licensing, support, security, and fraud prevention. We do not sell personal data or use Google user data for advertising.

Macro scripts and automation data are stored locally by default. Data is sent to third-party services only when you explicitly configure and use an optional integration, such as Discord webhooks or an AI provider. Those services process data under their own privacy policies.

Authentication and licensing records may be retained while your account or license remains active and for a limited period afterward where needed for security, legal, or support purposes. To request access, correction, or deletion of your account data, email hotrantentien98@gmail.com.

We apply reasonable safeguards, but no storage or transmission method is completely secure. SmartMacroAI is not directed to children under 13. Material policy changes will be published in this repository with an updated effective date.
